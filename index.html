How it works

From a first challenge to reviewed work.

  1. Plan your route

    Choose a track, mark the skills you already have, and see the challenges to take at your pace.

  2. Build and submit

    Build the challenge and submit it with a link. A mentor scores each skill from 1 to 4 against written descriptions.

  3. Prove it

    Skills scored 3 or higher are verified and added to your passport. After your work is approved, you review a peer's work too.
