How it works

From a first challenge to reviewed work.

  1. 1

    Choose a track and mark the skills you already have. The route builder shows the challenges to take and how long they take at your pace.

    Plan your route
  2. 2

    Build the challenge and submit it with a link. A mentor scores each skill from 1 to 4 against written descriptions.

    Build and submit
  3. 3

    Skills scored 3 or higher are verified and added to your passport. Once your work is approved, you review a peer's work too.
